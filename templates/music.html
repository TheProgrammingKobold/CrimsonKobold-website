Music!

Music has always been a really important part of my life. Even as a kid I loved hearing and repeating melodies I had heard from the radio or our mp3 player.
Fast forward to my teen years and I'm starting to really pick out my own music. At first it was very internet core with like dubstep, electroswing, fnaf music (smh) (But id still listen to them to this day so smh my head x2)
And I remember making playlists on youtube or downloading these songs through youtube to mp3 websties where I put them on my phone!
I had also been introduced into midi keyboards for my 13th birthday when my parents bought me one for me to play around with. I didnt know how to use DAW's at the time so I was just really messing around.
Fast forward to 2020, and I had really started taking this seriously. My dad bought FL studio wanting to try it out but was like meh and gave it to me. (But also he just wanted an excuse to get it for me im sure.)
Anyway, I've been practicing in FL for years now, going through several phases like most artist do I'm sure. But now I'm at a point where I'm really starting to enjoy the music I make.
Before it always seemed like I couldn't get past the idea that it was somehow bad, but I think it was just because I was a beginner, which I think prevented me from actually liking it.

BUT NOW

I'm havin a blast! Recently picked up learning the Harmonica too! Been super fun to stretch out your musical abilities I think. And I've also been doing a lot of 24 hour challenges on discord server!
It's run by this cool lad called Sammy, and its Sammy's Electro-Indie Lounge. But its much more than just a lougnge we got games and dnd and questions of the day and movie night
Yeah its a cool place for music lovers to hangout. And that's basically it =3c
