About Me!

Hello! This is my website where I'll share maybe aspects of my life or projects I've been working on!!! I'm happy to contribute to making the web more indie by bringing back real soul to it. At least I think so anyway.
My name online is Rem or Crimson or Spicy! Just pick any of those and I'll probably respond!!! I'm a huge nerd basically, never been too good at being social but who is these days. Seems like everyone says that these days. I kinda wish I was an extrovert but its okay, maybe if I was this place wouldnt exist! And it makes me happy =3

I was born in the early 2000's, so I'm a gen Z, but getting older each day it feels like. I grew up in the ds, xbox 360 era, and have fond memories of going to places like toys r us on my birthday each year to buy legos!
I have huge influnces from early days of technology and internet (Early for me anyway). I loved flash games and also CD rom games I got to play growing up!
I remember poptropica, or armor games, or those play 100 free games websites hehe. But also I had CD roms of hot wheels beat that, and bookworm adventure, and bejeweled 3 and twist!
This was also the early stages of phone app games, which I played on my moms iphone whenever I could. Very fond memories!

I was banned from watching cartoon network as a kid, but I did watch a lot of spongebob or disney movies when I could! I remember watching them on DVDs even! Which I'm a big physical media guy.
I love how to train your dragon sooo much!!! Its one of my big influences on me as a child. I remember being depressed toothless wasnt real after watching it because i wanted friends sigh...

I also alawys had an interest in computers and technology. I loved learning about minecraft and modding it, its what inspired me to learn about coding and stuff. So without it I wouldn't be writing this right now!

Now I'm an adult going to college for computer science! Which has been pretty fun, excited to see if I do anything interesting in my life. But besides that I mostly just work on my porjects like music or games and such.
I also love making music and find it really enjoyable. Im a huge nerd for synths or audio production in general. I've been using fl studio for about 6 years now to try and make music. And while its not the best music that's ever existed, its my music and I like it =3

Anyway, that's about it for now. I might adjust this and add more later but we'll see.
